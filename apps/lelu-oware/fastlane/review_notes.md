**Purpose and audience.** Lelu Oware is Oware, the traditional Ghanaian mancala game, for iPhone and iPad, for anyone who loves the game or wants to learn it, with the feel of a carved Ghanaian board. Play the computer at four levels (Novice to Grandmaster), a friend on the same device, the Journey (a series of opponents), daily riddles, and a short lesson; two rule sets (Abapa and Nam-Nam).

**Setup and main features.** No account, login or setup is needed. From the menu: play the computer, play a friend on one device, the Journey, riddles and the lesson. Settings has the rules, sound and haptics, the switch for anonymous usage statistics, and links to the privacy policy and support. Game Center is optional.

**Paid content.** The only purchases are three optional tips (consumable In-App Purchases) under Support. They unlock nothing; the whole game is free.

**External services.** Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for the optional tips; Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements, if the player signs in to it; TelemetryDeck (EU) for anonymous usage statistics, only after the player agrees (off until then, and can be switched off in Settings). No accounts, advertising or tracking.

**Regional differences.** None: the app works the same in every region. It is in English; tip prices follow Apple's regional pricing.

**Regulated industry or third-party material.** Not applicable. Oware is a traditional public-domain game; all artwork is original, made for this app; Adinkra symbols are used with their meanings, respectfully. No licensed third-party content.
