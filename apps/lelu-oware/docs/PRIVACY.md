# Lelu Oware — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

Lelu Oware is a single-player and pass-and-play board game for iPhone and iPad.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Lelu Oware does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account,
no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK.

## What stays on your device

- Your current game, riddle progress, Journey stars and settings are saved only on your device
  (and in your device backups, if you have those enabled).
- Tips are optional in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details and
  keep only a count of tips on your device so the app can say thank you.

## Game Center (future versions)

If a future version adds Game Center leaderboards or achievements, they will use Apple's Game Center
service under Apple's privacy policy. We will not display your Game Center player ID, and we will
update this policy before that feature ships.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
