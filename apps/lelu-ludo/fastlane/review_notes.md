**Purpose and audience.** Lelu Ludo is the board game Ludo as it is played in Ghana, for iPhone and iPad, for families and players of all ages who know Ludo and want the Ghanaian house rules they grew up with (back kicks, side kicks, home kicks, two tokens forming a wall, the black-star 6), or who want to learn the game. Play against the computer at four levels (Novice to Grandmaster) or with friends passing one device; a seven-lesson tutorial teaches the game on the real board. The board and pieces are original artwork in the style of a carved Ghanaian wooden board.

**Setup and main features.** No account, login or setup is needed. From the menu: Start Game (choose your colour, 1–3 computer opponents and a level, then Play; tap the dice cup to roll and a token to move; when a token has more than one move, such as a kick, each choice is shown on the board); Friends (2–4 people on one device); Learn (seven short lessons with scripted dice); Settings (rules: Ghana Classic, Classic or Custom house rules; sound and haptics; links to the privacy policy and support); Support (an optional tip jar).

**Paid content.** The only purchases are three optional tips (consumable In-App Purchases) under Support. They unlock nothing; the whole game is free.

**External services.** None apart from Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for the optional tips. No accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs; the game does not use the network. The game and settings are stored only on the device.

**Regional differences.** None: the app works the same in every region. It is in English; tip prices follow Apple's regional pricing.

**Regulated industry or third-party material.** Not applicable. Ludo is a traditional public-domain game; all artwork is original, made for this app; the Ghana flag colours and black star are used respectfully as part of the game's Ghanaian identity. No licensed third-party content.
