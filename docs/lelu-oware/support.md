# Lelu Oware — Support

Lelu Oware is a free game of Oware (Nam-Nam and Abapa rules) for iPhone and iPad, made in celebration of the
people who carve the boards and the players who gather round them.

## Need help?

Email **[trendnestorg34@gmail.com](mailto:trendnestorg34@gmail.com?subject=Lelu%20Oware)** and say which iPhone or iPad you use
and what happened. Screenshots help.

## Common questions

- **Seeds appear without moving.** Check iOS Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Motion. With Reduce Motion
  on, seeds still travel but without tumble or bounce. In the game's own Settings, check Sowing speed.
- **No sound.** The game follows your phone's Silent mode. Switch Silent off, and check Sound is on
  in the game's Settings.
- **How do I capture?** Your last seed must land on the other player's row and leave that house with
  two or three seeds. The full rules are in the app under More ▸ Rules & heritage.
- **Tips.** The optional tips in Settings unlock nothing; every chapter and board look is free.

## Privacy

No account and no ads. The game sends only anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off in Settings ▸ Share anonymous usage stats. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
