# Lelu Ludo — Support

Lelu Ludo is a free game of Ludo as it is played in Ghana, for iPhone and iPad. It uses Ghana's
house rules: forward, back, side and home kicks, walls, and the black star 6. Play the computer
(up to three opponents, Novice to Grandmaster), or play with 2–4 friends passing one device.

## Common questions

- **How do I learn the rules?** On the home screen, tap **Learn the game**. There are short lessons
  on the real board: your yard, moving, kicking, back kicks, walls and the black star 6.
- **I can't bring a token out.** Only a 6 brings a token out of your yard. In Settings, choose
  **Custom** and turn on **A 1 also brings a token out** if you play it that way.
- **My token won't move.** Two tokens of one colour on a square make a wall, and no token can pass
  it. If every move is blocked, your turn passes.
- **What do the rules settings mean?** **Ghana Classic** has every Ghanaian kick, and a 6 or a kick
  earns another roll. **Classic** is plain Ludo. **Custom** lets you switch each rule on or off. A
  change applies to your next new game, not the game in progress.
- **The 6 shows a black star.** That is Ghana's black star. It counts exactly as a 6.
- **No sound.** The game follows your device's Silent switch. Turn Silent off, and check that
  **Sound** is on in the game's Settings. **Haptics** can be turned on or off there too.
- **Where is my game?** Your game in progress is saved on your device. Tap **Continue your game** on
  the home screen. Lessons never replace your saved game.
