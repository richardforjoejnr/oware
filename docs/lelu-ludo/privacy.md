# Lelu Ludo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 October 2026_

Lelu Ludo is a game of Ludo as it is played in Ghana, for iPhone and iPad. You can play the
computer, play friends on the same device, or learn the game with a short lesson.

## The short version

- **Lelu Ludo collects no data.** It has no account or sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It
  does not track you across apps or websites.
- The app does not connect to the internet. Your game and settings stay on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your current game**, so you can carry on after closing the app.
- **Your settings**: the rules for new games (Ghana Classic, Classic or your own rules), and whether
  sound and haptics are on.

They are stored only inside the app on your device, and in your device backups if you have those
turned on. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

**How long they are kept, and deleting them.** We hold no data about you, so there is nothing we can
retain or delete. Your saved game is replaced when you start a new one. Deleting the app from your
device deletes your saved game and settings too.

## Permissions

Lelu Ludo does not ask for any permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or
notifications. Sounds follow your device's Silent switch.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## If this changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data, such as Game Center leaderboards or optional
anonymous usage statistics, this policy will be updated **before** that version is released. It will
say exactly what is sent, to whom, how long it is kept and how to turn it off. Anything optional
will stay off until you agree. The new version will be posted at the same address, and the date
above will be updated.
